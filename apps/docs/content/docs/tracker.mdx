The **Address Tracker** shows live trading activity for any wallet on Polymarket.

## How to Use

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add a Wallet
Click **Add Wallet** in the Tracker widget (open it from the bottom bar). Enter a wallet address and an optional name. Doji fetches the Polymarket profile name if one exists.

</Step>
<Step>

### Track Multiple Wallets
You can track several wallets at once. Their trades appear in a single activity stream.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sound Alerts
Turn on sound notifications to hear a cue when a tracked wallet trades. You can pick the sound and adjust volume.

</Step>
<Step>

### Jump to Markets
Click any trade in the tracker to go to that market's trading page.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
> The tracker is for information only. Always do your own research before following another trader.
</Callout>

## Other Ways to Add Wallets

- **Leaderboard** — Click the track icon next to any trader.
- **Profile cards** — Track a wallet from their profile hover card.
- **Trades tab** — Track any address from the trade history on a market page.
