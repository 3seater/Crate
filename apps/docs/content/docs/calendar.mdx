The **Calendar** shows market expiration dates on a monthly grid.

## Features

- **Expiration dates** — See which markets resolve on each day.
- **Browse months** — Navigate forward and backward.
- **Market list** — Click a date to see that day's expiring markets, sorted by volume.
- **Quick navigation** — Click a market to go to its trading page.

## How to Open

Click **Calendar** in the bottom bar. It opens as a floating widget.

<Callout type="info">
> For multi-outcome events, the calendar parses dates from the market question when the shared expiration date is unreliable.
</Callout>
