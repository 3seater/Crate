The trading terminal is where you analyze markets and place orders. Click any market from the Explore page to open it.

## Navigation

- **Search** — Press <Shortcut>Ctrl+K</Shortcut> to search for markets, events, or categories.
- **Header** — Shows the current probability, market status, and key dates.
- **Bottom Bar** — Open the **Address Tracker**, **Watchlist**, **Calendar**, **Activity**, and **Settings** from the bar at the bottom of the screen.

## Settings

Open Settings from the bottom bar to configure trading behavior and notifications.

### Trading

- **Market Order Type** — Choose between **FAK** (Fill and Kill) and **FOK** (Fill or Kill) for market-style orders. FAK fills as much as possible then cancels the rest; FOK requires a complete fill or cancels entirely.
- **Orderbook Flash Effects** — Toggle the visual flash animation on the orderbook when new orders arrive.

### Notifications

- **Display Toasts** — Toggle toast notifications on or off, and choose their screen position (six positions available).
- **Notification Sounds** — Enable or disable audio alerts, pick a sound from the built-in library, and adjust the volume.

<Callout type="info">
> Settings are stored locally in your browser and persist across sessions.
</Callout>
