Your **Portfolio** is the central hub for monitoring your trades and performance on Doji.

## Overview Cards

- **Portfolio** — The total value of your USDC balance plus current outcome token positions. Includes a hide/show toggle for privacy.
- **Available to Trade** — Your liquid USDC balance ready for new orders.
- **P&L Chart** — A sparkline showing your cumulative profit and loss over time, with period selectors: **1d**, **1w**, **1m**, and **All**.

## Tabs

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Active Positions** | Current holdings with market, outcome, average entry, current value, and P&L. |
| **Closed Positions** | Resolved or fully sold positions. |
| **Orders** | Open limit orders (GTC) that haven't been filled yet. Cancel resting orders from here. |
| **History** | Complete log of trades, redemptions, deposits, and withdrawals. Filterable by type and time range. |
| **Redeem** | Claim winnings from resolved markets — exchange winning tokens for USDC. |

## Deposit & Withdraw

The portfolio page includes quick-access **Deposit** and **Withdraw** buttons that open the [Deposit & Withdraw](/deposit-and-withdraw) flow.

<Callout type="info">
> Use the **Watchlist** to keep an eye on markets you've exited but might want to re-enter later.
</Callout>
