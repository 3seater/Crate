## Overview

Doji uses **pUSD** (Polymarket USD) on **Polygon** for trading. Your balance shows in the header after you [set up your wallet](/sign-in-and-wallet-setup). A built-in bridge moves funds between other chains and your Polygon wallet. The bridge converts deposits into pUSD automatically.

## Quick Access

- **Header buttons** — Click **Deposit** or **Withdraw** in the app header to open the bridge modal.
- **Onboarding** — The "Fund your wallet" step opens the deposit flow automatically.

## Deposits

1. Click **Deposit** in the header (or during onboarding).
2. Choose a **source chain** and **asset**.
3. Copy the **deposit address** shown and send supported assets to it.
4. Wait for the bridge to process — most deposits complete within minutes.

Your header balance updates when funds arrive.

<Callout type="info">
Some chains enforce a **minimum deposit** in USD. The bridge UI shows minimums when they apply.
</Callout>

## Withdrawals

1. Click **Withdraw** in the header.
2. Choose a **destination chain** and **token**.
3. Enter a **recipient address** and **amount**.
4. Review the **quote** (fees and estimated output).
5. Follow the on-screen steps to confirm.

Fees and settlement time vary by network. The UI shows a breakdown before you confirm.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to Do |
|---------|------------|
| **Deposit not arriving** | Leave the tab open — most deposits complete within minutes. Check that you sent a supported asset to the correct deposit address. |
| **Withdrawal failed** | Try a smaller amount — bridge liquidity pools can be temporarily exhausted. Wait a few minutes and retry. |
| **Below minimum deposit** | Check the minimum shown in the bridge UI (typically $2–$9 depending on chain). |

<Callout type="info">
Large withdrawals (over $50,000) may benefit from splitting into smaller amounts. If issues persist, see [Common Issues](/common-issues).
</Callout>
