The **Watchlist** lets you save markets you want to follow without holding a position.

## How to Use

- **Add a market** — Click the <Star className="inline h-4 w-4 text-primary" /> icon on any market card or in the trading terminal header.
- **Open the panel** — Click **Watchlist** in the bottom bar to see your saved markets with live prices.
- **Remove a market** — Click the star icon again.

Your watchlist is saved across sessions.

<Callout type="info">
> The watchlist is useful for tracking markets you've exited but might want to re-enter later.
</Callout>
