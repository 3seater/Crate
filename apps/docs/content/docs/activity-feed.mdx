The **Activity** widget shows a live feed of recent trading activity.

## Opening the Activity Feed

Click **Activity** in the bottom bar to open the feed. The widget displays a scrolling list of trades as they happen in real time.

## Dockable Panel

The Activity feed can be docked to the **left** or **right** side panels for persistent viewing while you browse markets or manage your portfolio.

## Trade Details

Each entry in the feed shows:

- **Market name** — The market the trade occurred in.
- **Side** — Whether the trade was a buy or sell.
- **Price** — The execution price.
- **Size** — The amount traded.
- **Timestamp** — When the trade was executed.

## Navigation

Click any trade in the feed to jump directly to that market's trading page.

<Callout type="info">
> The Activity feed updates in real time. Keep it docked to stay aware of market momentum while you trade.
</Callout>
